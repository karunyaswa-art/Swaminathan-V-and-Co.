About the firm

Financial understanding.
Business perspective.

Swaminathan V & Co. is a Chennai-based Chartered Accountancy and financial advisory practice built on professional rigour and nearly four decades of corporate experience.

We support businesses, individuals and management teams across accounting and financial reporting, taxation, audit, financial planning, corporate finance, internal controls and strategic business matters. Our approach combines technical understanding with practical commercial perspective.

The practice draws on experience across FMCG and consumer businesses, manufacturing, healthcare, technology and professional services.

Swaminathan

Chartered Accountant | Corporate Finance Professional | Independent Director

Swaminathan is a Chartered Accountant with nearly four decades of professional experience across finance, strategy, governance, risk management and corporate restructuring.

His career includes senior finance leadership and CFO positions at CavinKare, TeleDNA and CURA Healthcare, as well as advisory and investee-company experience with Peepul Capital Advisors. His work has spanned corporate finance, taxation, financial planning, treasury, internal controls, restructuring and strategic decision-making.
